# Button

Buttons for Stockwoven's own website and docs, not for partner apps.

- `sw-btn sw-btn--primary`: purple fill, white label (4.5:1). One per view: "Talk to us", "Read the docs".
- `sw-btn sw-btn--secondary`: the inverse of the ground (black on ivory, ivory on black).
- `sw-btn sw-btn--ghost`: 1px `line` outline, for low-priority actions.
- Labels in sentence case, a verb first. An arrow `→` may follow the label.
- The consumer provides the element (`<button>` or `<a>`) and the label.
