# StatCard

One big figure with a label and a source, for the website, partner decks and reports.

- Markup: `<div class="sw-stat"><span class="sw-stat__value">$2.8B</span><span class="sw-stat__label">Tokenized stock market cap</span><span class="sw-stat__source">Source · date</span></div>`.
- `sw-stat--inverse` marks the one figure that matters most in a row; its value turns purple.
- Always give the unit and the source. Never invent a figure.
- The consumer provides the value, label and source.
