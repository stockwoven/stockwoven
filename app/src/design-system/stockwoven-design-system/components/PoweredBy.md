# PoweredBy

The badge that marks Stockwoven inside a partner's product: the symbol plus "Powered by Stockwoven".

- Markup: `<span class="sw-powered"><span class="sw-mark" data-sw-mark="#101010"></span>Powered by <b>Stockwoven</b></span>`.
- Use the `brand-ink` symbol on light screens and `brand-ivory` on dark screens. When it has to blend into the partner's palette, use the gray symbol (`assets/Logos/stockwoven-mark-gray.svg`, #66675F).
- Once per flow: bottom of the investment screen or in the asset detail. Never in the header, tab bar or next to the partner's logo.
- Never recolor the symbol to the partner's brand color.
- The partner provides the placement and the screen; the badge itself is fixed.
