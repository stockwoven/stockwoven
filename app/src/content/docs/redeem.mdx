# Redeem.

Burn swINTC and take your share of both tokens. The vault never pauses redemptions.

## How a redemption works

Always open. You take your share of both tokens straight from the Meteora pool.

Each share removes exactly `liquidity_per_share` liquidity from the vault's position. cp-amm pays both tokens directly to the holder, rounding down against the redeemer.

## Slippage limit

The app accepts up to 0.5% less of each token than the quote. If the pool moves further, the transaction fails and nothing is burned.

## Redemptions are never paused

The vault's pause only stops deposits. Issuer powers are a separate risk: see [Risks & protections](/docs/risks).
