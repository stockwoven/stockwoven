# Hold.

Each swINTC is a fixed slice of the pool's liquidity. Its value is read from the pool account, not from an oracle.

## How swINTC is valued

Value per swINTC = liquidity per share × pool reserves ÷ pool liquidity.

In full:

```
liquidity_per_share × (token_a_amount / 10^dec_a + token_b_amount / 10^dec_b) / pool.liquidity
```

One token of either issuer counts as one share of stock.

## Where the growth comes from

Every conversion through the Meteora pool leaves a fee in its reserves, so each swINTC is backed by a little more stock. Compounding fees grow the reserves while the pool's liquidity stays the same. See [Fees](/docs/fees).

## Verify it on-chain

Every figure in the app can be checked against the pool account and the swINTC mint on Solana Explorer. The addresses are listed under [Contracts](/docs/overview#contracts).
