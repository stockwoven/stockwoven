# Glossary.

## sw token

The vault's token for one stock: the stock ticker with the lowercase `sw` prefix, such as `swINTC`. A pro-rata claim on the vault's liquidity.

## Issuer

A company that tokenizes a stock. Several issuers can tokenize the same stock; each version trades in its own pool.

## mINTCx and mINTC

Devnet test tokens that stand in for INTCx from xStocks and INTC from Backpack.

## DAMM v2

Meteora's constant-product AMM (cp-amm). The vault's liquidity sits in one DAMM v2 pool per stock.

## Compounding fee

The part of each conversion fee that DAMM v2 adds back to the pool's reserves. It raises the value of every sw token.

## Liquidity per share

The fixed amount of pool liquidity behind one share, set when the vault is created.

## Harvest

Sweeping the non-compounding part of the fee to the treasury. Anyone can call it.

## Treasury

The account that receives the protocol's cut of conversion fees.

## Slippage limit

The most a quote may move before the transaction fails: 0.5% for deposits and redemptions, 1% for conversions in the app.
