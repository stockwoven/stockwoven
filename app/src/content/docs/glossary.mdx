# Glossary.

## sw token

The vault's token for one stock: the stock ticker with the lowercase `sw` prefix, such as `swSPCX`. A pro-rata claim on everything the vault holds: its liquidity in every pool and its idle reserves.

## Issuer

A company that tokenizes a stock. Several issuers can tokenize the same stock; each version trades in its own pools. SpaceX is tokenized as SPCXx by xStocks, SPCX by Backpack and SPCXon by Ondo.

## Mock SPCXx, SPCX and SPCXon

Devnet test tokens with the same symbols and decimals as the three issuers' SpaceX tokens. They stand in for the real ones on devnet.

## DAMM v2

Meteora's constant-product AMM (cp-amm). The swSPCX vault holds liquidity in four DAMM v2 pools.

## Compounding fee

The part of each pool fee that DAMM v2 adds back to the pool's reserves. It raises the value of every sw token.

## Idle reserve

A vault-owned token account for one token, holding the balance that isn't in a pool. It stages deposits, redemptions and keeper trades, and funds arbitrage. Each share owns its pro-rata slice of it.

## Keeper

The bot that signs the vault's rebalances, reserve swaps and arbitrage cycles. It can't mint shares or move funds out of the vault.

## Target weight

The keeper's target share of the vault's value in one pool, stored on-chain as `target_weight_bps`.

## Arbitrage cycle

A chain of 2 to 4 swaps that starts and ends in the same token. The program only lets it land if the vault ends with more of that token.

## Divergence loss

What a full-range pool gives up against simply holding its two tokens when their relative price moves.

## Harvest

Sweeping the non-compounding part of a pool's fee, the protocol's cut, to the treasury. Anyone can call it.

## Treasury

The account that receives the protocol's cut: 16% of pool fees and 20% of arbitrage profit.

## Slippage limit

The most a quote may move before the transaction fails. The app shows it before you sign.
