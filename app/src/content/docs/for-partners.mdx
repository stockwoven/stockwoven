# For partners.

Inside a neobank's app, the neobank's brand leads. Stockwoven stays small and consistent.

## Integration

The vault program is open: deposit and redeem are plain Solana instructions, and swaps go straight to the Meteora pools, so any router can fill them. Your customers can pay with USDC and get USDC back. A deposit from one token is two transactions, signed together: the swaps into the vault's mix, then the deposit. A redemption to USDC is one.

The [SDK](/docs/sdk) reads the vault, prices deposits and redemptions, and builds these transactions for your custody to sign. The integration guide and support contact are not published yet.

## The Powered by badge

<div className="doc-badge"><PoweredBy /></div>

- The symbol plus "Powered by Stockwoven". Place it once per flow, at the bottom of the investment screen or in the asset detail, never in the app's header or tab bar.
- Badge colors follow the partner's screen: ink version on light screens, ivory version on dark screens. Never recolor the symbol to the partner's color; if the badge must disappear into the partner's palette, use the one-color gray version.

## Tickers

Tickers keep the `sw` prefix in the partner app (`swAAPL`). The partner can style the chip in its own colors; the prefix and the monospaced face stay.

## Joint materials

Partner first, Stockwoven second: "[Partner] × Stockwoven", the partner's logo on the left, a 1px divider, Stockwoven's lockup on the right at the same height as the partner's logo.

Don't put the purple, the pattern or the full lockup inside a partner's product without their brand team's approval.
