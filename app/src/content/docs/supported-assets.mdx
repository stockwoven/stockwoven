# Supported issuers & assets.

Each stock gets one sw token, backed by every issuer's version. Tickers are the stock ticker with the `sw` prefix: `swINTC`, `swAAPL`.

## Assets

| Token | Stock | Issuers in the vault | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `swINTC` | Intel | xStocks and Backpack (devnet mocks) | Live on devnet |
| `swAAPL` | Apple | — | Not live yet |
| `swNVDA` | NVIDIA | — | Not live yet |
| `swTSLA` | Tesla | — | Not live yet |

swINTC is live on devnet. The others are not live yet, so they show no figures.

## Issuers

On devnet, test tokens stand in for INTCx from xStocks (mINTCx) and INTC from Backpack (mINTC).

**Token-2022 issuer tokens** need Meteora token badges, which INTCx and Backpack INTC already have.

## One pool per pair

There is one customizable pool per mint pair, ever. On mainnet, check that nobody created INTCx/INTC first, and choose token A/B on purpose: fees accrue in B.
