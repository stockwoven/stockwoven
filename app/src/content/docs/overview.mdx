# Overview.

One stock. One token.

Stockwoven is infrastructure on Solana. The same stock is tokenized by several issuers, and each version trades in its own pools. Stockwoven's vault holds every issuer's version, provides liquidity with it in Meteora pools, and gives back one yield-bearing token per stock.

## Who it is for

Stockwoven is offered mainly to neobanks and fintechs that want to give their customers tokenized stocks. The neobank owns the customer and the app; Stockwoven is the layer underneath.

## The three steps

- **Deposit.** Hand over the vault's current mix, or pay with one token: any issuer's SpaceX counts 1:1, and USDC or SOL is converted into the issuer token the vault needs most. Receive swSPCX.
- **Hold.** Each swSPCX is a pro-rata slice of everything the vault holds: its liquidity in four Meteora pools plus its idle reserves. Its value is read from the pool and reserve accounts, not from an oracle.
- **Redeem.** Burn swSPCX and take your slice of every token back, or receive it all as USDC. The vault never pauses redemptions.

Each step has its own page: [Deposit](/docs/deposit), [Hold](/docs/hold), [Redeem](/docs/redeem).

## Where the yield comes from

- **Pool fees.** Traders pay 0.30% in every vault pool, and most of it compounds for swSPCX holders.
- **Auto-balancing.** A keeper moves the vault's liquidity toward the pools that earn the most.
- **Arbitrage.** When the same SpaceX trades at different prices across pools, the keeper runs a swap cycle that only lands if the vault ends with more.

See [Fees](/docs/fees#where-the-yield-comes-from).

## What runs today

One vault on Solana devnet: swSPCX, for SpaceX. It holds liquidity in four Meteora DAMM v2 pools: SPCXx / SPCX, SPCXon / SPCXx, SPCX / USDC and SPCXx / SOL. Mock tokens stand in for SPCXx from xStocks, SPCX from Backpack and SPCXon from Ondo, with a test USDC.

Because two of the pools pair SpaceX with USDC and SOL, swSPCX is mostly SpaceX but not entirely. See [Hold](/docs/hold#what-swspcx-is-exposed-to).

Solana devnet only. Nothing here uses real funds.

## Live figures

The app reads its figures straight from the Meteora pools, the vault's reserves and the swSPCX mint on devnet. Nothing is cached or estimated.
