# FAQ.

## Where does swINTC's growth come from?

From conversions between issuers. Every conversion through the Meteora pool leaves a fee in its reserves, so each swINTC is backed by a little more stock. See [Fees](/docs/fees).

## Is there a price oracle?

No. cp-amm's own `L × reserve / L_total` rule prices deposits and withdrawals. See [How the vault works](/docs/how-the-vault-works#share-accounting).

## Can redemptions be paused?

No. The vault can pause deposits, never redemptions. Issuer powers are a separate risk: see [Risks & protections](/docs/risks).

## Can I deposit only one issuer's token?

Not yet. Deposits are proportional. See [Deposit](/docs/deposit#proportional-deposits-only).

## Is this on mainnet?

No. Solana devnet only. Nothing here uses real funds.
