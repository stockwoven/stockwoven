# FAQ.

## Where does swSPCX's growth come from?

From three sources: the 0.30% fee traders pay in the vault's four pools, the keeper moving liquidity toward the pools that earn the most, and arbitrage between pools that only lands when the vault ends with more. None is promised. See [Fees](/docs/fees#where-the-yield-comes-from).

## Is swSPCX 100% SpaceX?

Mostly, not entirely. The vault holds USDC and SOL inside its SPCX / USDC and SPCXx / SOL pools, so swSPCX's value is a mix of SpaceX, USDC and SOL. The app shows its USD value as 1 swSPCX = $X. See [Hold](/docs/hold#how-swspcx-is-valued).

## Is there a price oracle?

Not for the mix. Depositing the mix and redeeming in kind use no price: a share is a pro-rata slice of every position and every reserve, and cp-amm's own `L × reserve / L_total` rule prices the liquidity part. See [How the vault works](/docs/how-the-vault-works#share-accounting).

Depositing or redeeming one token values the vault in SpaceX shares: issuer tokens at 1:1, USDC and SOL at prices the keeper posts. Those trades stop if the prices are more than 10 minutes old or a vault pool trades more than 2% away from them. See [Deposit](/docs/deposit#when-a-token-is-not-accepted-alone).

## Can I deposit with just one token?

Yes: USDC, SOL or any issuer's SpaceX token, in one transaction. Issuer tokens count 1:1; USDC and SOL are converted into the issuer token the vault holds least of. See [Deposit](/docs/deposit#deposit-with-one-token).

## Can I get USDC back?

Yes. The vault pays one issuer token, which the app sells for USDC in the same transaction, or you keep that token. See [Redeem](/docs/redeem#receive-usdc).

## Can redemptions be paused?

No. The vault can pause deposits, never redemptions. Issuer powers are a separate risk: see [Risks & protections](/docs/risks).

## What can the keeper do?

Move liquidity between the vault's pools, swap between its reserves, run arbitrage cycles and post the prices single-token trades use. It is trusted to choose sizes and prices, but it can't mint swSPCX or move funds out of the vault, and every arbitrage must leave the vault with more. See [How the vault works](/docs/how-the-vault-works#rebalancing).

## How does Stockwoven make money?

From 16% of every pool fee, collected by `harvest`, and 20% of every arbitrage profit. Deposits and redemptions in one token pay a vault fee of up to 1% (or earn a small discount) that stays with holders; with the mix or in kind there is none. See [Fees](/docs/fees#protocol-revenue).

## Is this on mainnet?

No. Solana devnet only. Nothing here uses real funds.
